## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a luminous, high-precision aesthetic engineered specifically for an AI-powered ad agency. It transitions decisively into an ultra-clean, high-illumination light mode that pairs enterprise-grade clarity with kinetic, algorithmic vitality.

### Brand Personality & Demographics
- **Tone:** Visionary, razor-sharp, forward-looking, hyper-creative, dynamic.
- **Target Audience:** CMOs, VP-level creative leaders, high-growth brand architects, and enterprise performance marketers who demand generative velocity, intelligence, and premium visual execution.
- **Emotional Response:** Technical mastery, electric creative energy, pristine clarity, and unstoppable velocity.

### Visual Style Movement
The system merges **Prismatic Minimalism** with **Light Glassmorphism**. The UI replaces dark obsidian backdrops with a crisp, luminous white-to-slate ground layered with crystalline translucent surfaces, subtle silver-tinted borders, crisp typography, and high-energy accents of electric cobalt blue, vivid coral, and warm amber.

## Colors

The color system anchors itself in crisp whites, silvery slate layers, and high-contrast charcoal typography, punctuated by electric chromatic accents that signal generative intelligence and campaign conversion power.

### Roles & Palette Structure
- **Primary (`#1E40AF` / `#2563EB`):** Electric Cobalt Blue. Anchor for high-priority actions, verified AI execution states, and authoritative focal points.
- **Secondary (`#FF4D4D`):** Vivid Coral. Denotes creative generative pulses, campaign impetus, and conversion highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Radiant Amber. Provides warm contrast for telemetry metrics, status indicators, and creative experimentation badges.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate/Charcoal. Provides crisp, ink-like contrast for typography, high-priority iconography, and structural line work.

### Surfaces & Gradients
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Pristine, high-reflectance backdrop providing maximum legibility and lightness.
- **Canvas Subtle (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft cool off-white for full-bleed section striping and nested panels.
- **Luminous Kinetic Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #1E40AF 0%, #2563EB 45%, #FF4D4D 100%)`. Applied to flagship hero text, gradient borders, and promotional ribbons.
- **Sunburst Acceleration Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF4D4D 0%, #F59E0B 100%)`. Reserved for creative performance metrics, AI generation sparks, and conversion tags.

## Typography

The typography unites the geometric, forward-leaning character of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced, humanist neutrality of **Manrope**.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Space Grotesk:** Deployed for all major display headlines, card titles, key metric numbers, and interactive chips. Tightly tracked uppercase labels (`label-md`) impart a calibrated, machine-like precision.
- **Manrope:** Drives long-form campaign briefs, prompt inputs, ad metadata descriptions, and editorial body text. Its high legibility ensures zero visual fatigue over bright surfaces.
- **Contrast Integrity:** Body text defaults to deep slate (`#1E293B`) on white surfaces, never receding past `#64748B` for secondary notes to ensure strict WCAG AAA contrast compliance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a fluid, structured 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of 1440px to ensure expansive, breathable editorial compositions.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) margins. Content scales naturally within fluid wrappers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Side-by-side prompt and generation inspectors stack into tabbed or accordion panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Multi-column showcase cards compress into swipeable carousels or single-column stacks.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on deliberate micro-to-macro contrast: tight 4px–12px gaps within control clusters, paired with generous 64px–96px section padding that lets light, airy spaces showcase complex generative assets.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than heavy, dark shadows that dirty the canvas, depth is established through **Crystalline Glass Layers**, **Low-Contrast Outlines**, and **Prismatic Ambient Diffusion**.

### Elevation Scale
1. **Base Ground:** Pure `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Cards & Modules):** Semi-opaque `#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a crisp perimeter border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
3. **Floating UI (Dropdowns, Floating Navigation, Popovers):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or frosted glass with a luminous, dual-tier shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06)`.
4. **Active & Hover States:** Subtle -2px physical translation paired with a micro-glow tinted in electric blue (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`) or vibrant coral (`rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined medium-radius geometry (`roundedness: 2`). This delivers crisp architectural order without feeling austere or rigid.

### Corner Radius System
- **Compact UI (Buttons, Inputs, Toggles):** 8px (`0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Panels & Preview Boxes:** 16px (`1rem`).
- **Hero Banners & Floating Modals:** 24px (`1.5rem`).
- **Status Tags & Prompt Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between interactive tags and content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Cobalt Blue (`#1E40AF` to `#2563EB`) solid fill, white text, 8px radius, height 44px (desktop). On hover: shifts slightly brighter (`#1D4ED8`) with an energetic light shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Frosted white ground (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`), text `#0F172A`. On hover: border tints to `#2563EB` and text shifts to `#1E40AF`.
- **Coral Impulse Button (Conversion / Generate):** Solid Coral (`#FF4D4D`), crisp white text, accompanied by a subtle pulsing coral shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.28)`).

### Chips & Filter Badges
- **Form:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), 12px `Space Grotesk` uppercase text, `letter-spacing: 0.05em`, vertical padding 4px, horizontal padding 12px.
- **Style:** Light slate tint (`#F1F5F9`), border `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, text `#475569`. Active chips transition to solid cobalt blue fill with crisp white text.

### Cards & Creative Containers
- **Anatomy:** Frosted surface with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, 1px outline in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, 16px border-radius.
- **Hover Motion:** Vertical lift of -3px, shadow expanding softly with a slight blue or amber tint, and an optional 1px gradient border illumination.

### Input Fields & Prompt Editors
- **Base Style:** High-clarity white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px outline (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`), 48px height, 16px horizontal padding.
- **Typography:** 14px `Manrope`, placeholder styled in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Outline transitions to Electric Blue (`#2563EB`) with a clean 3px diffuse focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** Checkboxes use a 4px soft radius; radio buttons are circular.
- **Unchecked:** 1.5px border (`#CBD5E1`), background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checked:** Solid Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`) or Coral (`#FF4D4D`) fill, featuring an ultra-crisp white checkmark or center pip.

### Ad Metric & AI Generation Tiles
- **Structure:** Crisp white cards featuring a prominent numeric readout in `Space Grotesk` (e.g., `+184% ROAS`), accompanied by a micro badge in soft green or radiant amber (`#F59E0B`) to highlight algorithmic optimization gains.
